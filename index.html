How this works

The page asks NEA's open data service for hourly PM2.5 (tiny smoke particles) and the 24-hour PSI, one number per region. For AQI it converts PM2.5 to a 0 to 500 score with the US EPA scale, then picks a band and an otter. The big number is the average of the five regions.

Band names for AQI are read off screenshots of the Haze@SG app, so cut-offs may differ slightly. The island outline is simplified Natural Earth data. Data: api-open.data.gov.sg/v2/real-time/api/pm25 and …/psi.
